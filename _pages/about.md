I am a PhD Candidate in Economics at the [University of Strathclyde](https://www.strath.ac.uk/business/economics/).

My research focuses on **applied macroeconomics, international macroeconomics and finance, and Bayesian macroeconometrics**, with a particular interest in geopolitical risk and its macro-financial transmission. I use **Vector Autoregressions (VARs), Bayesian VARs, and Time-Varying Parameter models** to study how macroeconomic and financial shocks propagate across countries and evolve over time.

My doctoral research is supervised by Dr Sharada Nia Davidson, Dr Ping Wu, and Prof. Gary Koop.

Before joining Strathclyde, I completed an MSc in Mathematical Economics and Econometrics at the University of Edinburgh and a BA (Hons.) in Finance at Zhejiang University of Technology.

## Featured Working Paper

### The Transmission of Global and Country-Specific GPR: International Evidence from 50 VARs

*with Sharada Nia Davidson*

This paper examines how the geographic scope of geopolitical risk shapes its international macro-financial transmission. Using more than 50 country-specific Bayesian VARs, we find that global GPR acts predominantly as an inflationary supply disturbance, while country-specific shocks generate more heterogeneous inflation and monetary-policy responses. Cross-border adjustment is most systematic at the regional level, with markedly different transmission patterns across Asia, North America, and Europe.

*Presented at the Scottish Graduate Programme in Economics (SGPE) Conference, Crieff, 2026.*
